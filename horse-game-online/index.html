🐴 Horse World

A real-time horse simulation game

🎮 How to Play:

  • 👆 Click on the map to move your selected horse
  • 🐴 Click a horse name on the right panel to select it
  • 🍎 Use action buttons to Feed, Pet, or Rest your horse
  • 👀 Watch the stats - horses need food, rest, and attention
  • ⚡ Keep them happy! - lonely horses get sad
Click on horses or the map to start playing!
