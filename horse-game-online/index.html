🐴 Horse World Adventure

Ride horses, explore, and have fun!

🎮 How to Play:

  • 🐴 Pick a horse and click "Mount Rider" to get a stick person on it
  • 🚴 Auto-Move - Rider walks around on the ground! Adjust speed with slider
  • 🛑 Stop Movement - Click to make the rider stop walking
  • 🍎 Feed, Pet, Rest - Keep your horse healthy and happy
  • 👤 Dismount - Get the stick person off the horse
  • 🌳 Avoid trees on the ground!
Select a horse and click "Mount Rider" to start!
